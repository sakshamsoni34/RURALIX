## 📑 SLIDE 2: Problem Statement & Rural Challenges

### 🎨 Visual Layout Suggestion:
- 4-Card Grid or Split Layout (Left: The Reality Stats, Right: 4 Pain Point Cards with warning icons).

### 📝 Slide Content:
#### The Ground Reality:
- Over **63 Million MSMEs** exist in India; **51%+ are in rural & semi-urban areas**.
- **Over 70% of new rural micro-enterprises fail within 2 years** due to unvalidated assumptions and rapid cash depletion.

#### Critical Gaps Faced by Rural Founders:
1. **Hyper-Local Information Blind Spots:**
   - No data on localized market demand, nearby competitor saturation, or local supply-demand voids.
2. **Lack of Feasibility Stress-Testing:**
   - Businesses are started based on guesswork without calculating fixed capital runway or power/infrastructure risks.
3. **Underutilized Government Subsidies:**
   - Schemes like **PMEGP (up to 35% subsidy), MUDRA, Stand-Up India** remain unavailed due to complex paperwork and lack of awareness.
4. **Digital & Language Literacy Divide:**
   - Existing advisory tools & ERPs are English-first, desktop-heavy, and too complex for non-tech-savvy villagers.
5. **No Daily Operational Blueprint:**
   - Founders lack step-by-step Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs) and supply chain guidance.

### 💡 Highlight Box:
> ⚠️ **Key Problem:** *"Rural entrepreneurs don't fail because they lack hard work; they fail because they lack hyper-local data, feasibility testing, and structured financial guidance."*
